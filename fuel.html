Straight talk

Carbs are fuel. Use them like it.

30 g of carbs is about 120 calories. That is what makes Fuel work, and it is why you should know this:

  • Fuel is built for hard sessions of an hour or more. For a short, easy workout, water may be all you need.
  • Cutting weight? Count Fuel in your daily intake.
  • If you have diabetes or manage your blood sugar, talk to your doctor before using Fuel.

We built Fuel to make you better. Using it right is part of that.

Debrief

Questions, answered directly.

Why HBCD instead of regular sugar?

Highly branched cyclic dextrin is a carbohydrate built for training. It is easy on the stomach mid-workout and gives steady energy without the spike and crash of dextrose.

Is Fuel a pre-workout?

No. It is an endurance drink you sip during training. Take Core+ or Core− before, and Fuel during.

Can I use it for hiking, hunting or long days outside?

Yes. Anytime you are working hard for an hour or more, Fuel helps keep your energy steady.
