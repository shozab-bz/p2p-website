Subscriptions

A subscription ships every 30 days at $59.99 a bag. Shipping is free on orders of $98 or more. Pause, skip or cancel anytime from your account; changes made before a renewal processes apply to that renewal.

Returns

Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method. Write to team@prime2performance.com with your order number and we will send the return steps.

Damaged or wrong items

If an order arrives damaged or wrong, write within 14 days with a photo and we will replace it or refund it. No need to ship anything back.

Founder orders

Founder pricing is locked for life and capped at two bags of each formula a month. Founder orders follow the same return terms as every other order.
