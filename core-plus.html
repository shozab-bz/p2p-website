Straight talk

Respect the caffeine.

200 mg is about two cups of coffee. Enough to train hard, not enough to wreck your night, if you time it right.

  • Count the rest of your day’s caffeine, coffee included.
  • Skip it within 6 hours of bed. Training late? Use Core− instead.
  • If you have high blood pressure, a heart condition, or take prescription medication, talk to your doctor first.

We built Core+ to make you better. Using it right is part of that.

Debrief

Questions, answered directly.

Will it make me jittery?

200 mg is a moderate dose, about two cups of coffee. If you are sensitive to caffeine, start with half a sachet or try Core−.

Why glycerol?

Glycerol draws water into your muscles. That supports hydration and a fuller pump through long, hard sessions.

How is Core+ different from Core−?

The same full stack. Core+ adds 200 mg of caffeine, 150 mg of theobromine and 100 mg of L-theanine for calm, steady focus.

What is the tingling?

Beta-alanine. It is harmless and fades within about 30 minutes.
