More questions, answered

Who is this built for?

Men training seriously from about 45 on. Nothing in the formulas is age-specific. They are dosed for men who train hard and read labels.

Core− or Core+?

The base formula is identical. Core+ adds 200 mg of caffeine, 150 mg of theobromine and 100 mg of L-theanine. Choose Core− if you train in the evening, avoid caffeine, or already have your coffee.

Do I need all five?

No. Each formula is complete on its own. Most men start with the Training Pair, Core and Fuel, and add from there.

How does a subscription work?

It ships every 30 days at $59.99 a bag, $10 off list, with free shipping on 2 bags or more. You can pause, skip or cancel at any time from your account. Discounts do not stack.

Should I check with my doctor?

If you have a medical condition or take medication, yes. Take the full ingredient list to your physician. We can tell you what is in each formula and how much, but we can't advise on your own health.
