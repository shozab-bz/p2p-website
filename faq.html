The system

The system

Who is this built for?

Men training seriously from about 45 on. Nothing in the formulas is age-specific. They are dosed for men who train hard and read labels.

What is the 2nd Prime?

The years from about 45 on, when the base you built and the judgment you have now work together. We make the nutrition for training through it.

Do I need all five formulas?

No. Each formula is complete on its own. Most men start with the Training Pair, Core and Fuel, and add from there.

Which formulas can I buy now?

P2 Core−, P2 Core+ and P2 Fuel. Reveille and Repair arrive Q1 2027, and you can ask to be notified when they ship.

Why sachets?

Each sachet is one full, measured serving. Nothing to scoop or weigh, and it travels wherever you go.

The formulas

The formulas

Core− or Core+?

The base formula is identical. Core+ adds 200 mg of caffeine, 150 mg of theobromine and 100 mg of L-theanine. Choose by the time of day and how you handle caffeine.

What is in each formula?

Everything on the label, with its dose. We use no proprietary blends, which are mixes that hide individual amounts.

What is the tingling from Core?

Beta-alanine. It commonly causes a brief tingling of the skin. It is harmless and fades within about 30 minutes.

Should I check with my doctor?

If you have a medical condition or take medication, yes. Take the full ingredient list to your physician. We can tell you what is in each formula and how much, but we cannot advise on your own health.
