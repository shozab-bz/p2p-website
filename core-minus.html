Why It Exists

Caffeine-Free For Late Training.

More than one in three American adults aged 50 to 64 sleeps less than 7 hours a night (CDC, 2024). Caffeine stays in your system for hours after your last set. Train after work on a stimulant pre-workout and you pay for it at 2 a.m.

You do not need a stimulant to train hard. You need blood flow, endurance and hydration. P2 Core− delivers all three, so you can train whenever life allows and recover overnight.

Questions, Answered Directly.

Core− or Core+?
The base formula is identical. Core+ adds 200 mg of caffeine, 150 mg of theobromine and 100 mg of L-theanine. Choose by the time of day and how you handle caffeine.
What is the tingling?
Beta-alanine. It commonly causes a brief tingling of the skin. It is harmless and fades within about 30 minutes.
Should I check with my doctor?
If you have a medical condition or take medication, yes. Take the full ingredient list to your physician.
