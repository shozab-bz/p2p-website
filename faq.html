The Formula

Core− or Core+?

Choose one for the day—they are alternatives, not a pair.

P2 Core+ is the stimulant option: the full Core stack plus 200 mg of caffeine for sessions that need more. P2 Core− is the stimulant-free option: the same full-dose stack with zero caffeine, built for late training days and easy sleep.

Pick the one that fits the day in front of you. Training early and want the push? Core+. Training after work, or keeping caffeine out of your afternoon? Core−. Use one or the other on a given day—not both.

Whichever Core you choose, pair it with P2 Fuel during the session to complete the Training Pair.

What is the tingling from Core?

P2 Core− and P2 Core+ each contain beta-alanine, an ingredient that can cause a temporary tingling sensation known as paresthesia.

The tingling is generally harmless when caused by beta-alanine, although individual experiences vary, and it typically fades within about 30 minutes.

For the best experience, always check the product label and follow the recommended serving instructions. If the sensation is severe, persistent, or accompanied by other symptoms, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.
